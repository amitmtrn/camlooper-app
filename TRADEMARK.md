# Trademark and brand policy

[The license](LICENSE) covers CamLooper's **source code**. It does not grant any rights to
the project's name or branding. Those are reserved.

## Not covered by the license

- The names **CamLooper** and **Camlooper**, and confusingly similar variations
- The CamLooper logo and application icons (`public/favicon.ico`, `public/app-icon.png`,
  `public/splash-screen.png`, `src-tauri/icons/`)
- The domains `camlooper.com`, `dl.camlooper.com` and `ads.camlooper.com`
- The Microsoft Store listing and the identifier `com.camlooper.app`

## What you may do

- Say your project is "based on CamLooper" or "a fork of CamLooper", as a factual statement
- Use the name in articles, tutorials, reviews, comparisons and academic work
- Keep the branding intact when you build the app **unmodified** for your own use

## What you may not do

- Publish or distribute a modified build under the name CamLooper, or under a name likely to
  be confused with it
- Use the logo or icons as the identity of your own project or product
- Suggest your fork is endorsed by, affiliated with, or the official version of CamLooper

**If you distribute a modified version, rename it and use your own icons.** Change
`productName` and `identifier` in `src-tauri/tauri.conf.json` and replace the icon set. This
keeps users from mistaking your build for an official release — which matters because
official releases are the ones that get security updates through
[camlooper.com](https://camlooper.com), the APT repository and the Microsoft Store.
